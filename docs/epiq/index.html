Mission

EPIQ carries WOAH’s epidemic intelligence and much more: disease situation reports, ad-hoc analyses and the integration of surveillance data. It combines official data reported by Members with non-official sources scouted by WOAH, such as online media and data sources identified for rapid risk assessment.

Where we are going

Integrated intelligence

Official and non-official data, unified across WOAH.

Anticipatory risk intelligence

From event detection to trends, foresight and modelling.

Decision support, at scale

Feeding WOAH risk assessments, powered by automation and AI.

Highlights of the year

  • Data scouting: mapping and assessment of data sources for rapid risk assessment, globally and with a regional focus.
  • Situation reports: streamlined production, soon published on a public Git page (this site).
  • Horizon scanning: online media data integrated for early detection of emerging threats.
